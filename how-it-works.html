How tree-service requests work

A clear starting point for homeowners and property managers in Jacksonville and nearby Onslow County communities.

Before you start

What this site is — and is not

Jacksonville Tree Removal is an independent local request resource run by New River Digital. We are not a tree company: we don’t climb, cut, or haul anything, we don’t dispatch emergencies, and we don’t guarantee that a provider will be available for your request. What we do is simpler — we give your tree problem a clear first step, review the basics with you, and explain the available next steps honestly.

Immediate danger comes first

For downed power lines, life safety, or an immediate hazard, call 911 or the appropriate utility first. Do not rely on this website — or our phone line — as emergency dispatch. This site accepts non-emergency requests only.

The process

Three steps, no guesswork

1. Describe what’s happening

Choose the closest match — a tree needs to go, branches need attention, a stump is in the way, storm cleanup, clearing space, or “help me find a starting point” — then add your town and a few details. No tree names or technical terms needed. A photo from your phone helps, taken from a safe distance. Never approach an unsafe tree.

2. We review the request

New River Digital follows up to understand the request: the type of work, the location, access limits, and any safety concerns. This review is free. Submitting a request doesn’t book a crew, promise a quote, or guarantee a provider will be available — it starts a conversation.

3. You decide what’s next

If a suitable provider option is available, we explain the connection before sharing your details — and only with your okay. The provider then handles its own quote, contract, payment, and work directly with you. You are never obligated to hire anyone because you sent a request.

Set expectations

What you can count on from us

Free to request

Sending a request costs nothing. There is no fee, no deposit, and no payment step anywhere in this process.

No obligation

A request is not a booking and not a promise of availability. You can walk away at any point, no explanation needed.

Consent before sharing

We use your details to follow up about your request. We will tell you before sharing them with any provider — and we never sell personal information. See our privacy information.

Plain-English help

You don’t need to diagnose the tree or know the terminology. “Tall pine leaning toward the house since the storm” is a perfectly good starting point.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a tree company?

No. Jacksonville Tree Removal is an independent request resource run by New River Digital. We don’t perform tree work, climb, cut, or haul anything — we review requests and explain the available next steps.

Does it cost anything to send a request?

No. Sending a request is free and doesn’t commit you to any work or any provider.

How quickly will someone follow up?

New River Digital reviews each request and follows up to understand it. We don’t promise a specific response time, and submitting a request doesn’t guarantee a provider will be available.

Will my information be shared with a tree company?

Only with your knowledge. We use your details to follow up about the request, and we explain the connection before sharing your details with any provider. We don’t sell personal information.

What if my situation is an emergency?

For downed power lines, life safety, or an immediate hazard, call 911 or your utility company first. This site accepts non-emergency requests only and doesn’t provide emergency dispatch.
