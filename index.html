GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
get started.

Are you the tree company?

No. This is an independent request resource operated by New River Digital. We review the request and explain available next steps. Any tree company you choose is responsible for its quote, contract and work.

Does it cost anything to send a request?

No. Sending a request is free and does not commit you to tree work. Any service price comes from the provider after they assess the job.

What if I’m not sure what the tree needs?

Choose “Help me find a starting point” in the form. Describe what you notice from a safe place. We cannot diagnose tree health or assess a hazard remotely.

Will my information be shared?

We use your details to follow up about your request. We will tell you before sharing them with a provider. Read our privacy information.

What if there are power lines or immediate danger?

Stay clear and call 911 or your utility. Do not wait for this form or our phone line. We do not provide emergency dispatch.
